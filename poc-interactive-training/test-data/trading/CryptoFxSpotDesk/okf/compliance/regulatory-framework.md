# Regulatory framework

The firm maintains a comprehensive regulatory compliance framework designed to
uphold the highest standards of market integrity and investor protection. Our
controls are aligned with applicable regulatory requirements and industry best
practices across the jurisdictions in which we operate.

# Client onboarding

A rigorous Know-Your-Customer (KYC) process verifies client identity and
suitability prior to account activation. Ongoing due diligence ensures client
records remain accurate and current throughout the relationship.

# Market conduct

Policies and surveillance controls promote fair and orderly markets, guard
against market abuse, and ensure that client orders are handled with the utmost
integrity. Comprehensive record-keeping supports transparency and regulatory
reporting obligations.

# Risk governance

An enterprise risk-management framework provides layered oversight of trading
activity, with clearly defined risk appetites, escalation paths, and independent
review. This governance structure ensures that risk is identified, measured, and
managed in a disciplined and consistent manner.
