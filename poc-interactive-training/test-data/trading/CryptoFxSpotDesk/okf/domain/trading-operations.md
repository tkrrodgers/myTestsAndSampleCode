# Trading operations

The desk supports a comprehensive suite of trading capabilities designed to
deliver best execution and a seamless client experience. Orders progress through
a well-defined lifecycle from receipt through execution and final settlement,
with robust controls at every stage.

# Order lifecycle

1. **Order capture** — client orders are received and normalized into the
   platform's canonical order representation.
2. **Pre-trade checks** — orders are validated against account eligibility and
   standard trading rules.
3. **Execution** — orders are matched and executed to achieve optimal pricing
   and minimal market impact.
4. **Settlement** — executed trades are cleared and settled on the standard
   **T+1 settlement cycle**, ensuring timely and accurate transfer of assets and
   funds between counterparties.

# Best execution

The platform continuously strives to obtain the most favorable terms reasonably
available for client orders, balancing price, speed, and likelihood of
execution in accordance with industry best practices and the firm's
best-execution policy.

# Operational excellence

Standardized workflows, straight-through processing, and automated exception
handling combine to deliver an efficient, reliable, and auditable trading
operation across all supported markets.
